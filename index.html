Guide

How Ratings Work

Overall

Tap 1–5 stars for how you rate the strain. Overall is optional and stays separate from Effects and Side Effects. In My Score, the app shows your average star rating; Community balances each Lab Rat equally.

Effects

What the weed actually did. One dot = noticeable. Two dots = strong. These describe the experience and never change the Overall star rating.

✨EuphoricLifted, happy or unusually good mood.
🎯FocusedEasier to concentrate or stay locked in.
🎨CreativeIdeas, imagination or making things flowed.
🎉SocialMore talkative, open or comfortable with people.
😂GigglyLaughed more easily or found things funnier.
⚡EnergizedMore alert, active or motivated to move.
🧘RelaxedCalm or loose without necessarily feeling sleepy.
🛋️Couch-LockedHeavy-bodied, planted or hard to get up.
🍕HungryMore appetite or serious munchies.

Side Effects

Generally undesirable reactions, using the same two-dot intensity.

😴SleepyDrowsy or ready for sleep.
😰AnxiousWorried, tense or uncomfortable.
👀ParanoidSuspicious, unsafe-feeling or spiraling thoughts.
😶‍🌫️WashedFoggy, depleted, cooked or not useful.
💤KO’dUnexpectedly put to sleep or fully out.
💫DizzyLightheaded, spinning or unsteady.
🤕HeadacheHead pain or pressure.

Lab Results

Longer bars mean an Effect or Side Effect was reported more consistently or more strongly. Green = Effects. Red = Side Effects.
