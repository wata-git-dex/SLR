Your SLR quick guide

Three places to explore: Strains, the Lab, and your Profile.

Strains

The main collection opens to the community. Use See your strains to see your own shelf. Open a card for its details, ratings and reported effects. Cards and List change the layout.

Log a session

Blaze opens session logging. Select a strain and record what you experienced. Overall stars are optional; effects and side effects stay separate. Add Strain adds a specimen to the collection.

The Lab

Open Lab for community stats. Lab Stats shows community activity and the Lab Rats; Results shows shared patterns; Lab Tests shows sessions.

Your Profile

Open Settings for a quick profile summary. Tap My Lab to open your full profile. View achievements takes you to My Lab, with level, XP, average rating and a separate Badges collection.

Make it yours

Settings → Appearance contains mode, menu material, selected navigation and your account’s available accents.
