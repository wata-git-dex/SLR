Mix Experiments

  • ⚗
    Lab → Mixes → New Experiment

    Choose 2–4 existing batches. Each gets an equal share; check the brand when names match.

  • ◎
    Add your Lab Rats

    Share the recipe. Each invited Rat adds their own stars, effects and notes from My Mixes → View Mix.

  • ★
    Save your result

    A mix has its own rating. It never changes individual strain ratings or normal Session totals.

+10 XPFor each mix you record a result for.

First Blend · +25 XPYour first mix result.

Mix Notes · +35 XPResults for 5 mixes.

Shared Bench · +25 XPA mix with results from at least 2 Lab Rats.

Mix XP adds to your existing profile total. Invitations alone earn no XP. Collection and Checklist include mix badges; History shows new confirmed unlocks, newest first. Unsaved mixes remain as a draft on this device.
