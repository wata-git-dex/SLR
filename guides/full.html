The SLR guide

SLR connects your strain collection, shared Lab results and personal progress.

1. Strains and your shelf

The main feed shows community strains. Switch to your own shelf with See your strains, or return to Community. Search and filters narrow the collection; Cards and List change its presentation. Open a strain to see batches, who tried it, ratings and effects. Type colors distinguish Sativa, Hybrid and Indica; they do not indicate rating quality.

Newest ordering follows purchase history. Adding an Overall rating to an older session does not make its strain a new purchase.

2. Logging

Add Strain adds a specimen. Blaze opens session logging for an existing strain. Record Overall (1–5), effects and side effects separately. One effect dot means noticeable; two means strong. Save before leaving the form.

3. Ratings

Your strain score averages your rated sessions for that strain. Community scores give each member’s average equal weight. Profile and My Lab averages use your rated sessions. Unrated sessions are excluded, never treated as zero. Effects and side effects never become an Overall score.

A dash or “Unrated” means no Overall rating is recorded. If backfilling an older session in Notion, update its Rating field from your own record of the experience, then refresh SLR. Keep its original dates and links.

4. The Lab

Community Stats summarizes shared activity. Lab Stats contains the Lab Rats’ profiles, sessions, strains and averages. Results summarizes reported patterns. Lab Tests lists sessions, newest first, including recorded ratings. My Lab holds your personal progress.

5. Profile and badges

Settings includes a quick profile summary; tap My Lab for your full profile. View achievements opens My Lab. The profile card shows level, XP, strains, sessions, badge count and average rating. The separate Badges card opens Collection or Checklist; tap a badge for its requirements.

Activity XP is 10 per session plus 5 per distinct strain. Badge XP is added separately. High Voltage, Dreamwalker and Best of Both gain another rank every five distinct qualifying strains, with 25 XP per rank. Complete Observation gains a rank every ten qualifying sessions, with 35 XP per rank. Other badges remain one-time achievements. Repeated ranks use the same badge artwork. Refreshing never awards the same progress twice.

6. Appearance and navigation

Appearance offers Dark or Light mode, Glass or Solid material, Black or Accent selected navigation, and the accent choices available to your account. Lab tabs switch views. Lab, Add Strain and Blaze are actions; their placement adapts to screen width.
