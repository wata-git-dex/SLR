Strains & your shelf

  • ▦
    Community collection

    The app opens to the shared collection. See your strains switches to your shelf.

  • ⌕
    Search & filters

    Narrow the collection. Cards and List change its layout.

  • ↗
    Open a specimen

    See batches, who tried it, stars, effects and terpenes.

Sativa, Hybrid and Indica colors describe strain type. Newest order follows purchase history; rating an older session leaves that order intact.

Add a strain & log a session

  • 1
    Add Strain

    Enter the name, brand, type and THC. Add country, terpenes and a photo when available.

  • 2
    Blaze

    Select an existing strain and its batch.

  • 3
    Record your experience

    Choose optional Overall stars, then effects and side effects. One dot means noticeable; two mean strong.

  • 4
    Log Session

    Save the session and wait for confirmation. The collection refreshes after saving.

Adding a strain creates its specimen and batch. Blaze records your session with it.

Stars, effects & averages

★ OverallOptional whole stars from 1 to 5.

● / ●● EffectsNoticeable / strong. These never turn into stars.

— UnratedNo saved Overall score. Excluded from averages.

  • ①
    Your strain score

    The average of your rated sessions for that strain.

  • ②
    Community score

    Each member’s average gets equal weight.

  • ③
    Profile average

    The average of your rated sessions across strains.

Past sessions: In Notion, update the session’s Rating field from your record of the experience, then refresh SLR. Keep its original date and batch link.

XP & achievements

Activity XP+Badge XP=Total XP

+10 XPPer saved session.

+5 XPPer distinct strain tried.

+ Badge XPEarned from achievement requirements.

Community cards show Activity XP. Your profile adds badge XP to show the total used for your level.

Badges that gain ranks

BadgeNext rankXP per rank
High Voltage5 new distinct sativas25
Dreamwalker5 new distinct indicas25
Best of Both5 new distinct hybrids25
Complete Observation10 qualifying sessions35

Ranks reuse the same artwork. Other badges are one-time achievements. Refreshing doesn’t award the same progress twice. Ratings-based badges use saved Overall stars.
